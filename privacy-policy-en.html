Privacy Policy – DogCare

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Information Collected

DogCare does not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, location, or any directly identifiable data. The app may automatically collect non‑personal technical information required for proper functionality:

  • device model
  • Android version
  • app performance data
  • anonymous ANR and crash reports (if provided by the Android system)

This information is used solely for diagnostics and improving app stability.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DogCare uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • ad interaction data
  • anonymous technical device information

This information is used for:

  • serving ads
  • frequency capping
  • fraud detection
  • personalized ads (only with user consent)

For users in the EU, DogCare uses Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) to manage GDPR consent.

Google policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9805023

3. Data Stored on the Device

DogCare may store user‑entered information locally on the device, such as:

  • dog activity logs
  • appointments
  • notes
  • reminders
  • other information added by the user

This data is not transmitted to external servers and remains exclusively on the user’s device.

4. Data Sharing

DogCare does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. The only external service used is Google AdMob, in accordance with Google’s policies.

5. Security

DogCare does not collect sensitive data. Local information is protected using standard Android security mechanisms. The user has full control over all data stored within the app.

6. User Rights

Users may:

  • change ad consent settings through Google UMP
  • uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data
  • reset the Advertising ID from device settings

DogCare does not retain any data after the app is uninstalled.

7. Children

DogCare is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect personal information from minors. The app is designed for adult users only.
