Privacy Policy – DogCare

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information Collected

DogCare does not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, location, or any directly identifiable data. The app may automatically collect non‑personal technical information required for proper functionality:

  • device model
  • Android version
  • app performance data
  • anonymous ANR and crash reports (provided by the Android system)

This information is used solely for diagnostics and improving app stability.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DogCare uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • ad interaction data
  • anonymous technical device information

This information is used for:

  • serving ads
  • frequency capping
  • fraud detection
  • personalized ads (only with user consent)

For EU users, DogCare uses Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) to manage GDPR consent.

3. Data Stored on the Device

DogCare may store user‑entered information locally on the device, such as:

  • daily activities
  • vet appointments
  • notes
  • reminders
  • dog information
  • food items checked in the “Can my dog eat this?” menu

All data remains exclusively on the user’s device and is not transmitted to external servers.

4. Camera and Gallery Access

DogCare may use the device camera to allow the user to take photos that are saved inside the app. The user manually chooses when and what photos to capture.

DogCare also allows the user to select photos from the device’s gallery. Selected images are used only inside the app, stored locally on the device, and:

  • are not uploaded to external servers
  • are not analyzed
  • are not shared with third parties

DogCare does not use the camera in the background, does not collect biometric data, and does not perform facial recognition.

5. Data Sharing

DogCare does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. The only external service used is Google AdMob, according to Google’s policies.

6. Security

DogCare does not collect sensitive data. Local information is protected using standard Android security mechanisms. The user has full control over all data stored in the app.

7. User Rights

Users may:

  • change ad consent settings through Google UMP
  • uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data
  • reset the Advertising ID from device settings

DogCare does not retain any data after uninstallation.

8. Children

DogCare is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect personal information from minors.
